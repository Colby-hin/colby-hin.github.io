BACKGROUND

About Me

Cybersecurity felt like a natural fit for me because it matches how my mind operates. I've always enjoyed looking at problems from different angles, considering different perspectives, and working through complex challenges. What drew me in even more was realizing how much I could learn independently through building home labs, running simulations, and developing projects that allow me to put concepts into practice.

Over the past couple of years, I've made a point of learning something new and improving my skills every day. The more I've explored the field, the more certain I've become that this is the career I want to pursue long term. My goal is to bring that same curiosity and dedication to a full-time cybersecurity role, and until then, I'll continue building my experience through hands-on work.
